Nimoli Bhandari Events for macOS

INSTALL
1. Extract the ZIP on the Mac and open the extracted "Nimoli Bhandari Events" folder.
2. Move "Nimoli Bhandari Events.app" to Applications or another folder you control.
3. Control-click the app in Finder and choose Open. Confirm Open if macOS warns that
   the app is from an unidentified developer. The app is not currently signed or
   notarized with an Apple Developer ID.
4. Choose "Open RSVP dashboard" in the launcher window.
5. On first run, select "Create organizer account" in the dashboard.
6. Create an event before adding guests.
7. Choose "Exit Nimoli Bhandari Events" when finished.

Use the Apple Silicon download on Macs with an M-series processor. Use the Intel
download on Intel Macs. The app and its data are stored separately from the Windows
edition.

DATA
Guest records remain on this Mac in:
~/Library/Application Support/Gather RSVP/gather.sqlite3

Event photos and videos remain under:
~/Library/Application Support/Gather RSVP/media

Keep the Mac account and data folder private, and arrange backups for real event data.
The app starts with an empty guest list and does not transfer data from another
computer. Do not copy the SQLite database while the app is running.

LIMITS
- This is a local desktop app. It does not share or synchronize data with another
  computer or phone.
- Public guest RSVP links and WhatsApp campaigns require a separately hosted HTTPS
  website and official WhatsApp Business Platform setup.
- Imported contacts default to no WhatsApp consent. Only record consent after a
  guest has agreed.
- Do not collect Aadhaar/passport numbers or identity document images in this app.
